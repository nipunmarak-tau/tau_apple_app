# Privacy Policy for Tau Orchard Scanner

**Last updated:** October 26, 2023

This Privacy Policy describes how **Tau Orchard Scanner** ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

We collect information to provide and improve our App's features, specifically for orchard management and data analysis.

### A. Location Data (GPS)
*   **Data Collected:** Precise and approximate location data.
*   **Purpose:** To create GPX tracks that map your path through the orchard while recording video. This allows you to spatially visualize your data.
*   **Background Usage:** The App may collect location data even when the app is in the background if you have started a recording session, to ensure the GPX track is complete.
*   **Sharing:** Location data is stored locally on your device in `.gpx` files. We do not share this data with third parties unless you explicitly export or share the files yourself.

### B. Camera and Audio
*   **Data Collected:** Video and audio recordings.
*   **Purpose:** To record visual and auditory data of orchard rows for analysis.
*   **Sharing:** Recordings are stored locally on your device.

### C. Storage and Media
*   **Data Collected:** Photos, videos, and files stored on your device.
*   **Purpose:** To save your recordings and to display them in the App's dashboard for review and management.

### D. Device and Usage Data (Analytics)
*   **Data Collected:** Device model, operating system version, crash logs, and usage metrics.
*   **Purpose:** We use third-party services (such as Google Firebase) to monitor App stability, identify bugs, and improve performance.
*   **Sharing:** This technical data is shared with our analytics providers solely for development and maintenance purposes.
